Fire Lister Chrome extension and website

Privacy Policy.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Fire Lister helps dealership salespeople prepare Facebook Marketplace vehicle listings from ACV MAX inventory and manage price and availability changes on connected listings. This policy explains what Fire Lister handles to provide those features.

Information we collect and use

  • Account and preferences. Supabase receives your email address and password when you register or sign in, plus a display name if you provide one. The extension stores session tokens in Chrome to keep you signed in. Fire Lister stores your dealership association, description preferences, and any achievement badges and related notifications.
  • Vehicle and listing information. On supported ACV MAX showroom pages, Fire Lister identifies the vehicle and dealership. It retrieves inventory information such as VIN, specifications, price, availability, and photo URLs. The extension temporarily stores draft data in Chrome and uses it to fill the Facebook Marketplace vehicle form.
  • Marketplace activity. The extension detects navigation to relevant Marketplace pages and reads information needed for Smart Match, listing status, and verification. Connected records may include Facebook and MAX listing identifiers, a dealership identifier, listing title, thumbnail URL, price, and status. Fire Lister uses these records to match listings, surface alerts, and record completed changes. The extension refreshes alerts in the background while signed in.
  • Optional AI descriptions. If you enable AI descriptions, the extension sends vehicle details, including VIN when available, and your description preferences and custom instructions to the Fire Lister API. The API receives your session token for authentication and uses OpenAI to generate the description. Avoid including personal information in your custom instructions.
  • Website visits and support. Cloudflare processes requests to deliver the website, including network information such as IP addresses. This site includes no Fire Lister analytics scripts or advertising trackers. If you email support, Fire Lister receives the address, message, and any information you choose to share.

Who receives information

  • Supabase provides Fire Lister authentication and database services for accounts, preferences, connected listings, alerts, and achievements.
  • ACV MAX supplies dealership and vehicle data, including photos served by its photo host.
  • Meta receives details and photos entered into Facebook Marketplace. You review new drafts before publishing. Fire Lister also assists with price updates and marking listings sold when you start those actions.
  • NHTSA vPIC receives a VIN when the extension uses it to fill missing vehicle specifications.
  • Oracle Cloud and OpenAI support optional AI descriptions. Oracle Cloud hosts Fire Lister’s API, which sends the vehicle and writing inputs needed to generate the description to OpenAI.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and serves badge artwork.

Fire Lister may also disclose information when required by law or as necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Fire Lister’s use and transfer of information received through the Chrome extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Fire Lister uses extension data to provide, maintain, and improve its listing features. Fire Lister does not sell this data or use it for personalized advertising, lending, or credit decisions.

Retention and your choices

Draft and workflow data are stored temporarily in Chrome. Account information, connected listings, settings, and related records are stored on Fire Lister’s services as needed to operate the service and meet legal and security obligations.

You can switch to static descriptions in extension settings to stop sending inputs for AI descriptions. You can sign out or remove the extension to stop using it. For access, correction, or deletion of Fire Lister account data, email support@firelister.app. Manage Marketplace listings and your Facebook account through Facebook.

Changes and contact

Fire Lister will update this page when its privacy practices change. For privacy questions, contact support@firelister.app.
